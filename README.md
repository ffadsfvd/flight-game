Solvara Skies is an island flight career over the Solvara Archipelago, an invented chain of sun-soaked islands that each look their own: the main island of Marenza with its towns of painted houses on street grids round their squares, farms with crop rows, hedgerows and tractors on the lanes, harbours with moored boats and lit international airport, volcanic Calvera with black sand and lava flows, the red rock mesas of Orsa Cay, green Lumeno with its lagoon, lighthouse and wind farm, snow-capped Monte Nesta and the white sand of Coral Keys. Seven airfields range from the international airport to grass strips on a mountain terrace and a reef key, a seaplane lagoon and a runway deck on an offshore platform, with island traffic in its operators' colours (club Wrens in the circuits, a seaplane on the lagoon, crop dusters over the fields, the mail plane, a coast-guard jet), tower and unicom calls, and wind that changes from island to island, with ridge lift and thermals to soar in. You fly seven aircraft built in code, the Wren trainer, the Heron amphibian on floats, the Kestrel aerobatic biplane, the Pelican twin-engine island hopper, the Swallow motor glider, the Swiftlet jet and the Saker pylon racer, each with its own flight model (lift from airspeed and angle of attack, drag, thrust, stalls, flaps, gear, brakes, tailwheel or nosewheel handling, fuel burn, engine heat and damage) and a built 3D cockpit. Thirty-nine missions in six chapters (flight school, island work, rescue service, racing and airshow, night and storm, the expedition) cover circuits by day and night, canyon and mountain ring courses, a mountain pass crossing in a cold front with ice building on the wings, short-field, mountain, water, storm and platform landings by day and by night, a rescue take-off from a mountain terrace, heavy and multi-leg cargo with a fuel stop, a medevac against the clock, supply drops, firefighting, photo surveys, sightseeing tours with paying passengers who rate the ride on a comfort meter (steep banks, hard pulls and fast descents upset them), banner towing, a night search for a smuggler's boat, thermal soaring with the motor off, a dead-stick glide, jet and biplane aerobatics, formation flying and air races against AI rivals, from club Wrens round the mesas to a 200-knot pylon sprint along the beach, each ending in a debrief with a landing grade breakdown, medals, pay and ranks, plus a board of twelve challenges and a logbook. Play it in desktop Chrome with keyboard and mouse; everything, including the sound and music, is generated in code with no network requests.
