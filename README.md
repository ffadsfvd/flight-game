Solvara Skies is an island flight career over the Solvara Archipelago, an invented chain of sun-soaked islands that each look their own: the main island of Marenza with its towns, farms, harbour and lit international airport, volcanic Calvera with black sand and lava flows, the red rock mesas of Orsa Cay, green Lumeno with its lagoon, lighthouse and wind farm, and snow-capped Monte Nesta. You fly five aircraft built in code, the Wren trainer, the Heron amphibian on floats, the Kestrel aerobatic biplane, the Pelican twin-engine island hopper and the Swiftlet jet, each with its own flight model (lift from airspeed and angle of attack, drag, thrust, stalls, flaps, gear, brakes, tailwheel or nosewheel handling, fuel burn, engine heat and damage) and a built 3D cockpit. Twenty-eight missions in six chapters (flight school, island work, rescue service, racing and airshow, night and storm, the expedition) cover circuits by day and night, canyon and mountain ring courses, short-field, water and storm landings, heavy and multi-leg cargo with a fuel stop, a medevac against the clock, supply drops, firefighting, photo surveys, banner towing, a dead-stick glide, jet and biplane aerobatics, formation flying and air races against AI rivals, each ending in a debrief with a landing grade breakdown, medals, pay, ranks and a logbook. Play it in desktop Chrome with keyboard and mouse; everything, including the sound and music, is generated in code with no network requests.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts (then open http://localhost:8080)
```
